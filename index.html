What "street legal" actually buys you

A plate changes where you are allowed to ride.

This is the single biggest difference between the Lightning MK.II and a fast e-bike or an unregistered moped — and the reason the $3,999 price is worth understanding properly.

1

It plates and insures as a motorcycle

The Lightning MK.II is DOT-approved and ships with a 17-digit VIN, an MCO and complete registration documentation. You register it, you get a plate, and you buy normal motorcycle insurance for it — the same as any gas bike.

2

You need a motorcycle licence

Because it is a motorcycle and not a moped or a scooter, riding it on public roads requires a motorcycle licence — an M1 endorsement in California. If you do not have one yet, factor the course and test into your timeline.

3

Lane splitting is legal in California

As a plated motorcycle in California it may legally lane split, which is what turns a congested commute into a predictable one. Rules differ by state — check your local law before you rely on it.

Registration procedures, fees and licence requirements vary by state. HMP supplies the paperwork; the DMV process itself is yours to complete.

Common questions

Know before you order.

Do I need a motorcycle licence to ride it?
Yes. The Lightning MK.II is a motorcycle, not a moped or a scooter, so riding it on public roads requires a motorcycle licence — an M1 endorsement in California. If you do not have one yet, plan for the safety course and the DMV test before your bike arrives.
Can I register and insure it like a normal motorcycle?
Yes. It is DOT-approved and ships with a 17-digit VIN, the MCO and complete registration documentation. You register it at the DMV and insure it as a motorcycle. Registration procedures and fees vary by state.
How far does it really go?
The published maximum is 62 miles, measured at a steady 30 mph. In normal city riding — stop-and-go traffic, hills, some time near the 56 mph top speed — expect meaningfully less. Our working estimate is 40–48 miles, and because the battery is removable and charges from a normal 110V outlet, a mid-day top-up effectively doubles a day's usable range.
How do I charge it if I live in an apartment?
Lift the 74V pack out — it weighs about 40 lb — carry it inside, and plug the included 110V / 10 A portable charger into any household outlet. About 4.5 hours takes it from empty to full. No garage, no wall box and no electrician required.
Does it arrive assembled?
Yes — 100% assembled on arrival. Unlike some vehicles that need handlebar and front-panel installation, the Lightning MK.II is ready to ride out of the crate once you have your plate and licence.
Is it waterproof?
It is rated IPX6, which covers rain and road spray. As with any motorcycle, we recommend wiping water droplets off after riding in heavy rain, and not pressure-washing the electrical areas.
What routine maintenance does it need?
No oil changes, spark plugs, valve adjustments, fuel-system service or CVT belt and roller replacement. Routine maintenance focuses on tyres, brake pads and discs, brake fluid, suspension and steering inspections, plus periodic battery and controller checks. The single-sided swingarm and side-mounted motor make rear-wheel work considerably faster than on a hub-motor scooter.
Do I need an HMP dealer for normal service?
No. Any qualified motorcycle or powersports shop can handle normal mechanical work — tyres, brakes, fluids and safety inspections. HMP provides direct support for the battery, controller and vehicle electronics.
Should I buy this or the FLASH?
Buy the Lightning MK.II if you mostly ride city streets and arterials, your normal day is under about 35 miles, and price matters. Step up to the FLASH if sustained freeway speed is part of your route or you regularly need 70+ miles between charges. The comparison section above lays out both side by side.
What does shipping cost, and can I pick it up?
Vehicle freight is $350 within California and $500 elsewhere in the United States, and orders typically ship within 2 business days. Local pickup is free at our Davis, CA location (440 F Street) and our San Francisco, CA location (261 6th Street).
